To easily toggle Dark Mode and quick controls on Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome:

  1. Click the Extensions icon (🧩 Puzzle piece) on your browser's top toolbar.
  2. Find DMS Enhancer in the menu list.
  3. Click the Pin icon (📌) or eye icon so the extension stays visible for quick access.

💡 Key Features:

  • 🌙 Full Site-Wide Dark Mode: Seamless, eye-friendly dark theme designed for all pages across `dms.dilg.gov.ph` (Dashboard, Incoming, Received, Reports, Settings, etc.).
  • ⚡ Automated Bulk Actions: Perform mass actions including Bulk Accept, Bulk Route Internal (Beta), Bulk Complete, and Bulk Mark as Incomplete with built-in server throttle protection (Max 15 items per batch).
  • 👁️ In-Page Quick PDF Viewer: Review attached documents directly on the same page using a floating modal preview—no need to clutter your browser with extra tabs!
